White Water Rafting Company

We are committed to developing the community around us and 90% of our workers and guides are local to Southern Namibia. They have been brought up on the banks of the Gariep (Orange River) and can tell you many interesting facts about their home. The River Rafters also believes in keeping the river and river banks clean of dumping. Through the years we have conducted a couple of rafting excursions with our guides to clean the river and river banks and to remove rubbish. The River Rafters has a zero tolerance for dumping trash. We also do not allow any glass to go onto the river. Guests are expected to leave Namibia as they found it. This way you will ensure that the next person will experience Namibia as the absolutely beautiful jewel that it is.

History

We at The River Rafters have not been there at the start of the company. The Orange River White Water Rafting operations started in 1985. The original pioneers were Hugh Tucker from Aquatrails (then River Runners), Ross Murray (The River Rafters) and Felix Unite (Felix Unite Tourism group).

Paul started his Orange River white water rafting business in 1994. The then aim was to pay for his studies as student at University of Stellenbosch. After his degree, he worked 4 months for PWC before resigning to follow his newly discovered dream.
